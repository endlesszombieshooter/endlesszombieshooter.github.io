Patch Notes May 2025

v1.2 – New Enemy Type & Weapon Balancing

This update brings one of the most requested features: the Armored Zombie. This new enemy type soaks up a lot of damage and requires strategic weapon selection to take down efficiently.

What's new in v1.2:

  • New enemy: Armored Zombie — high HP, slow movement, drops rare loot

Balancing feedback is always welcome — if a weapon feels too weak or overpowered, let me know via the Contact page.

Gameplay Tips April 2025

How to Survive Past Wave 20: Advanced Strategies

Wave 20 is where most players hit a wall. The horde density increases significantly and zombie speed starts to outpace the average player's reaction time. Here are the strategies that consistently work.

Zone Control: Stop thinking about individual zombies and start thinking about zones. Define a "safe corridor" and funnel enemies into it. This dramatically reduces the number of directions you have to watch at once.

Weapon Rotation: Don't commit to one weapon. Swap between shotgun (crowd control), rifle (single targets), and pistol (conserving ammo between waves). Each serves a specific purpose.

  • Shotgun: Best for 3+ zombies in tight formation
  • Rifle: Best for fast runners at range
  • Pistol: Ammo-efficient for clearing stragglers

Health Management: At wave 20+, don't pick up health packs unless you're below 40% HP. You'll need them more later. Discipline here separates good runs from great ones.

Dev Update March 2025

Behind The Build: How Enemy AI Works

One of the most common questions I get is: how do the zombies navigate the map? The answer is a lightweight pathfinding system built from scratch in vanilla JavaScript.

Each zombie uses a basic steering behaviour — they move toward the player's position, but with a slight separation force to avoid clustering too tightly. This makes them feel like a natural horde rather than a single mass.

For the runner-type zombies, I added a look-ahead system where they predict the player's movement direction and cut off escape routes. This is what makes them feel genuinely dangerous at higher waves.

Future updates will introduce basic obstacle avoidance so the AI feels even more natural in complex maps. It's a fun technical challenge — stay tuned.

News February 2025

Endless Zombie Shooter Is Now Live

After several months of development, testing, and iteration — Endless Zombie Shooter is officially live on GitHub Pages.

What started as a small personal project turned into something I'm genuinely proud of. The core loop — move, shoot, survive, repeat — feels satisfying and the difficulty curve keeps runs engaging from wave 1 to wave 30+.

The game is completely free and will always remain free. No ads interrupting gameplay, no pay-to-win upgrades, no account required. Just open the link and play.

Thank you to everyone who tested early builds and sent feedback. This game is better because of you. Now — go see how long you can survive.
