Patch Notes May 2026

Update: Shop Upgrades, Coins & New Characters

This update introduces the full Shop system — earn coins in every run and spend them on permanent upgrades and new playable characters between sessions.

What's new:

  • Coins now drop from every zombie kill — collect them before they disappear
  • Shop: upgrade Damage and Mag Size for all 4 weapons (up to 5 levels each)
  • 4 new unlockable characters added alongside the default SGT. RIVERS
  • DET. HAYES (300 coins) — Speed +55%, lighter build
  • SWAT COLE (500 coins) — HP x2.3, Damage +45%, slower movement
  • CMDR. VOSS (800 coins) — Special Ops with a phase shift every 10 seconds
  • CAPT. RAMOS (1200 coins) — Rage passive: speed increases with kill count

Coins carry over between runs, so every session counts even if you die early.

Gameplay Tips April 2026

Weapon Guide: Glock, Mossberg, MP5 & M4A1

Dead City gives you 4 weapons to master. Each one fills a different role — knowing when to use which is the difference between surviving wave 15 and dying at wave 8.

  • Glock-17 (Pistol): Your starting weapon. 15-round mag, fast fire rate, zero spread. Reliable and ammo-efficient — great for picking off stragglers and conserving resources.
  • Mossberg (Shotgun): 6 pellets per shot, wide spread. Devastating at close range against grouped zombies but slow to reload at 1.8 seconds. Don't use it on Runners at range.
  • MP5 (SMG): 32-round mag, fires every 80ms. Best sustained DPS against regular and fast zombies. Burns ammo quickly — watch your reserves.
  • M4A1 (Rifle): Highest single-shot damage at 3.2x, nearly zero spread, long bullet range. The go-to weapon for Tank zombies. Slow reload at 1.9 seconds, plan your reload timing carefully.

Switch weapons with keys 1–4. Don't get caught reloading during a dense wave — swap to your pistol while the primary reloads.

Gameplay Tips March 2026

Know Your Enemies: All 4 Zombie Types Explained

Dead City has four distinct zombie types, and each one demands a different response. As waves progress, the harder types appear more frequently — here's what you're dealing with.

  • Normal Zombie: Standard walker. Slow, medium HP. Easy in early waves, dangerous in large groups. The Mossberg handles crowds of these well.
  • Fast Zombie (Runner): Smaller, much faster. Low HP but closes distance instantly. Always prioritise Runners before dealing with other zombies — they will reach you first.
  • Tank Zombie (Purple): Very high HP, large hitbox. Slow but hits hard on contact. Use the M4A1 — it's the only weapon that takes Tanks down efficiently without wasting an entire mag.
  • Exploder (Orange): Medium speed, explodes on death or when it reaches you. Keep your distance and never let one get close. Shoot it from range — never with the shotgun at close quarters.

From wave 8 onwards, all four types spawn together. Prioritise: Exploders → Runners → Tanks → Normals.

Dev Update February 2026

4 Arenas, 4 Stories: Designing Dead City's Maps

Dead City launched with four distinct arenas, each with its own layout, atmosphere, and backstory. Choosing the right arena is part of the strategy.

  • Police Precinct: Where it all started. Indoor layout with desks and lockers as cover. The default arena and the most forgiving for new players.
  • Downtown: The city burns. Large building columns as obstacles, open sightlines between them. Good for rifle users who want distance.
  • St. Mary's Hospital: Patient zero was here. Tight corridors formed by hospital beds and walls. High intensity — the MP5 shines here.
  • Riverside Cemetery: The dead rise twice. Gravestones as scattered cover. The most chaotic layout — Exploders are especially dangerous here due to limited escape space.

Each arena has a unique intro story and colour palette. Select your arena from the main menu before each run. Arena choice doesn't affect difficulty scaling — only the layout changes.

News January 2026

Dead City: Last Officer Standing — Now Live

After months of development, Dead City: Last Officer Standing is officially live on GitHub Pages — completely free, no download, no sign-up required.

The game launched with 4 arenas, 4 weapons, 4 zombie types, a full audio engine built from scratch using the Web Audio API, procedural pixel art sprites, a coin and shop system, and a local leaderboard.

Everything — rendering, audio, AI, physics, UI — is written in vanilla JavaScript with no external game engine. Just HTML5 Canvas and code.

This is a solo indie project built out of a genuine love for top-down shooters. It'll keep getting updated. If you have feedback, use the Contact page — I read every message.
