ABOUT THE GAME

SURVIVE THE HORDE

Dead City: Last Officer Standing is a top-down pixel art survival shooter built entirely in vanilla JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas. No engine. No download. Open the browser and play.

You are the last cop standing in a city overrun by the undead. Choose your arena, pick your officer, and see how long you can hold the line. Earn coins. Upgrade weapons. Unlock characters. Beat your score.

HOW TO PLAY

GET IN THE FIGHT

01
MOVE
WASD or Arrow Keys. Stay mobile — standing still is dying.
02
AIM & SHOOT
Mouse to aim. Click to fire. Switch weapons 1–4.
03
COLLECT
Coins, ammo, and health packs drop from every kill.
04
UPGRADE
Spend coins in the Shop to unlock characters and upgrade weapons.
05
SURVIVE
Each wave is harder. Score high. Top the leaderboard.
