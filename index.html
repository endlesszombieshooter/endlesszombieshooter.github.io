// About the Game

Survive. Shoot. Repeat.

Endless Zombie Shooter is a browser-based top-down survival shooter built for quick, intense sessions. You are the last line of defense against an ever-growing horde of zombies. Each wave is faster, stronger, and more relentless than the last.

The game is completely free and requires no download or installation — just open your browser and start playing. Built with HTML5 and JavaScript, it runs smoothly on any modern browser on desktop or mobile.

Whether you have 5 minutes or 5 hours, Endless Zombie Shooter delivers pure, unfiltered zombie-killing action. How long can you last?

// How To Play

Get In The Game

Master the basics in seconds. Survival mastery takes longer.

01
Move Your Character
Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move around the map. Stay mobile — a stationary target is a dead target.
02
Aim & Shoot
Aim with your mouse and click to fire. Line up your shots carefully — ammo matters in later waves.
03
Collect Drops
Defeated zombies drop ammo, health packs, and weapon upgrades. Don't leave them behind.
04
Survive Waves
Each wave cleared earns you points and pushes the difficulty higher. The goal is simple: survive as long as possible.

// Survival Tips

Stay Alive Longer

🔄
Keep Moving: Circling the horde is almost always better than standing still and shooting. Movement buys you time.
🎯
Aim for the Head: Headshots deal significantly more damage and conserve precious ammo in the later waves.
🏃
Prioritize Fast Zombies: Runner-type zombies close distance quickly. Always eliminate them before dealing with slower threats.
💊
Save Health Packs: Don't pick up health items unless you need them — they stay on the map until collected.
🔫
Switch Weapons: Different situations call for different weapons. Shotguns for crowds, rifles for single targets.
📐
Use Corners: Funneling zombies into narrow choke points is one of the most effective ways to control large hordes.
