// Why Contact?

I Want To Hear From You

This is a solo indie project and every piece of feedback helps shape the game. Whether you found a bug, have a great idea, or just want to share your high score — I'm listening.

🐛
Bug Reports
Found something broken? Please describe what happened, which wave you were on, and which browser you're using. Screenshots help enormously.
💡
Feature Requests
Have an idea for a new weapon, enemy type, or mechanic? I review every suggestion and many features have come from player feedback.
🎮
General Feedback
Loved a wave? Hated the difficulty spike? Think the weapon balancing is off? All feedback is valuable and genuinely read.
🤝
Collaboration
Artists, sound designers, or other developers interested in contributing to the project — reach out.
Response time: I typically reply within 2–5 days. This is a solo project, so during busy periods it may take a little longer. Every message gets a reply.
